# Privacy Policy for Suwaya (سُويعَة)

**Last updated: September 2026**

Abdallah Kaballo ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Suwaya mobile application. Suwaya is an open-source Islamic productivity and time-management app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

*   **Location Data:** Suwaya requires access to your device's location to accurately calculate astronomical prayer times and "Suwaya" periods. This data is processed locally on your device. We do not track your movements or sell your location data.
*   **Authentication Data:** If you choose to create an account or log in via Google Sign-In, we collect basic profile information (such as your email address) strictly for account synchronization across devices.
*   **Usage and Crash Data:** We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor app stability and collect anonymous crash reports. This helps us identify bugs and improve the app's performance.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The app utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Link to the privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:
*   [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
*   [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy)

## 3. Data Storage
Most of your personal data, including routines and tasks, is stored locally on your device using the Isar database. Synced data is securely hosted via Supabase. 

## 4. Open Source Transparency
Suwaya is an open-source project published under the GPLv3 License. Our source code is publicly available for auditing to ensure maximum transparency regarding how your data is handled.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by reporting an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/Abdallah-Kaballo/Suwaya
