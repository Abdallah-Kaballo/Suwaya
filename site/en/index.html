Open-source time project

Not just a different clock face: a different way to organize the day.

Suwaya is an open-source project and productivity app exploring a time model built around a cycle from Maghrib to the next Maghrib. The model divides that cycle into 24 virtual Suwayas anchored to calculated astronomical and prayer-time markers.

From Maghrib to the next Maghrib

Instead of organizing the model solely around 24 equal civil clock hours beginning at midnight, Suwaya builds a cycle around Maghrib, sunrise, and other time markers. The modeled night phase runs from Maghrib to sunrise, followed by the day phase until the next Maghrib.

24 virtual units

The model allocates 12 Night Suwayas and 12 Day Suwayas. Every Suwaya contains 60 virtual minutes; the amount of civil time those minutes occupy varies with the duration of the astronomical period.

Time and planning together

The app offers tasks, habits, routines, and focus sessions, plus prayer-time calculations and a Hijri calendar. Users can switch between Suwaya and civil time displays.

Clearly stated limits

Suwaya Time is a software-defined representation model, not an internationally adopted time standard. It does not change official civil time or replace religious authorities in matters of worship.
