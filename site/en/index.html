What are Suwaya and Suwaya Time?

Suwaya is an open-source software project and productivity application. It explores an alternative way to represent time and organize daily activities, called the Suwaya Time model, which relates the displayed time to an astronomical cycle and prayer-time calculations configured for a location.

Short definition

Definition of Suwaya Time

Suwaya Time is a virtual time representation that divides a cycle from Maghrib to the following Maghrib into 24 Suwayas: 12 Night Suwayas from Maghrib to sunrise and 12 Day Suwayas from sunrise to the next Maghrib. Each Suwaya contains 60 virtual minutes, but its duration in civil time varies with the astronomical period, location, date, and selected calculation settings.

Equal numbers of virtual Night and Day Suwayas do not mean that actual night and day are equal in duration. Suwaya Time is an alternative representation model, not an internationally adopted time standard or a replacement for official civil time.

How does the model work?

1. Set location and date

Geographic coordinates, date, time zone, and prayer calculation settings determine the temporal anchors.

2. Calculate astronomical anchors

Solar events and prayer times are calculated using the selected method. Results can differ across methods and settings.

3. Build the cycle

The model cycle runs from Maghrib to the next Maghrib, with a Night phase from Maghrib to sunrise and a Day phase from sunrise to Maghrib.

4. Map it to 24 Suwayas

The model allocates 12 Suwayas to each phase. The real duration of a Suwaya and the flow of its virtual minutes vary by period.

How is it different from civil time?

Civil time uses conventional clock hours. Suwaya Time maps a 24-Suwaya virtual cycle onto the astronomical cycle from Maghrib to Maghrib. Every Suwaya has 60 virtual minutes, which do not necessarily equal 60 civil minutes; therefore, virtual time can flow at different rates during different periods.

This is a model for time display and the organization of tasks, habits, routines, and focus sessions. It does not change official time and should not replace civil time for legal schedules, transport, or other purposes requiring an official clock standard.

What does the app offer?

Daily planning

Tasks, recurring habits, routines, and focus sessions, with civil and Suwaya time displays.

Prayer times and calendar

Prayer-time calculations using multiple methods and location settings, plus a Hijri calendar and night/day period tools.

Open-source time engine

The repository includes a Dart package for cycle calculation, Suwaya distribution, and mapping between civil and Suwaya time.

Inspect and contribute

The public repository contains the source, specification, and tests that can be reviewed and improved.

Frequently asked questions

Are the actual night and day equal in length?

No. The model assigns 12 virtual Suwayas to each phase. Actual night and day durations vary by location and season.

Is Suwaya Time an official standard or a UTC replacement?

No. It is an alternative time representation within an open-source project, not an internationally adopted standard and not a replacement for official civil time.

Do prayer-time calculations always produce identical results?

No. Results depend on coordinates, calculation method, Fajr and Isha angles, madhab settings, manual adjustments, and high-latitude rules.
